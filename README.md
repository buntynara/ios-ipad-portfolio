# Educational iPad Applications – Portfolio

A collection of interactive educational applications developed for preschool and elementary-age learners. These applications combine native iPad experiences, touch-based interactions, gamification, multimedia feedback and educational gameplay to make learning engaging and accessible.

## 1. GazziliPuzzles

**Category:** Preschool Educational Game

An interactive puzzle-solving application designed to develop early cognitive skills, spatial awareness and fine motor coordination.

**Key features**
- Drag-and-drop puzzle interactions optimized for iPad touchscreens.
- Gamified learning activities and self-paced challenges.
- Visual animations, sound effects and rewarding feedback.
- Child-friendly interaction patterns and engaging gameplay.

**Youtube Video:** [GazziliPuzzles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R2uNBmVUd6o)

## 2. GazziliShapes

**Category:** Preschool Geometry Game

An interactive geometry application that introduces children to shape recognition, sorting and spatial manipulation through playful activities.

**Key features**
- Interactive manipulation and arrangement of geometric shapes.
- Progressive challenges involving shape identification and spatial reasoning.
- Animated learning scenes.
- Achievement and sticker-based reward system.

**Youtube Video:** [GazziliShapes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fmIhG42p8m4)

## 3. GazziliScience

**Category:** Early Childhood Science Application

An interactive science application introducing foundational concepts such as plant growth, the water cycle, seasons, living and non-living things, and the five senses.

**Key features**
- Step-by-step interactive science activities and simulations.
- Exploration of concepts such as seed planting, plant development and sink-or-float experiments.
- Modular learning activities covering multiple science topics.
- Audio narration and contextual animations.

**Youtube Video:** [GazziliScience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bWebsJSgrl0)

## 4. Everyday Mathematics – Baseball Multiplication

**Category:** Educational Math Game

A baseball-themed multiplication game based on the Everyday Mathematics curriculum, helping students practise multiplication facts through competitive gameplay.

**Key features**
- Dynamic multiplication question generation and answer validation.
- Baseball-inspired outcomes such as singles, doubles, triples and home runs.
- Two-player turn-based gameplay.
- Inning tracking, strike counters and scoring.
- Interactive numerical keypad.

**Youtube Video:** [Baseball Multiplication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KXarFjldUkM)

## 5. Everyday Mathematics – Monster Squeeze

**Category:** Number Sense and Estimation Game

An interactive number-line game that develops number sense, estimation and logical reasoning through a hidden-number guessing challenge.

**Key features**
- Greater-than and less-than feedback to progressively narrow number ranges.
- Interactive number-line controls.
- Multi-round gameplay and score tracking.
- Large touch targets designed for young learners.

**Youtube Video:** [Monster Squeeze](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=roMa4Trw_NA)

## 6. Everyday Mathematics – Tric-Trac

**Category:** Mathematical Strategy Game

A dice-based strategy game that reinforces addition, subtraction and mathematical reasoning through number-matching challenges.

**Key features**
- Dice-roll simulation and sum calculation.
- Validation of number combinations against dice totals.
- Strategic number elimination and score optimization.
- Interactive controls and real-time score updates.

**Youtube Video:** [Tric-Trac](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=03ltC91cBsk)
